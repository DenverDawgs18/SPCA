MEDINA COUNTY SPCA — VOLUNTEER PORTAL MANAGER GUIDE
=====================================================
A plain-language reference for managers and administrators.
No technical knowledge required.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
Your volunteer portal lives at a separate URL from the public website.
Ask your IT contact or web developer for the exact address; it will look
something like:

    https://your-site.com/portal/login/

Bookmark that link. Use your staff username and password to log in.
If you forget your password, ask another manager or your IT contact to
reset it through the Django admin panel.

Once you are logged in you will see the Manager Portal — a dark sidebar
on the left and a dashboard in the middle. Every page shares the same
sidebar so navigation is always one click away.


DASHBOARD
---------
The first screen you see after logging in shows four numbers at the top:

  - Pending Applications  — volunteers waiting for review
  - Total Volunteers      — everyone in the system
  - Active Volunteers     — people considered currently active
  - Shifts Today          — shifts scheduled for today

Below the numbers is a table of recent hour logs and a row of quick-action
buttons. This is a good place to start each day.


APPLICATIONS
------------
When someone fills out the volunteer application form on the website, their
submission lands here. A red badge on the sidebar shows how many are waiting.

REVIEWING AN APPLICATION
  1. Click "Applications" in the sidebar.
  2. Click any applicant's name to open their full submission.
  3. Read through their answers and then choose one of two actions:

     Approve — Creates a volunteer account for the person and emails them a
     link to set their own password. Once they click the link and choose a
     password they can log in to the volunteer portal and sign up for shifts.

     Deny — Opens a text box where you can write a brief reason. That reason
     is included in the email sent to the applicant. Be respectful; they will
     read it.

     Both actions happen immediately when you click the button. There is no
     undo, so take a moment before clicking.

EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS FOR NEW APPLICATIONS
  Go to Settings (sidebar) and toggle "New application notifications" on or
  off. When turned on, you will receive an email every time someone submits
  a new application. Each manager controls their own preference independently.


VOLUNTEERS
----------
"Volunteers" in the sidebar shows every person who has been approved (or
imported). You can filter by Active, Inactive, or All, and search by name
or email.

VOLUNTEER DETAIL PAGE
  Click a volunteer's name to see their full profile:
    - Contact details pulled from their application
    - Total hours logged
    - Recent hour logs
    - Upcoming shift sign-ups
    - An internal Notes field (not visible to the volunteer)
    - An Active / Deactivate toggle

ACTIVATE / DEACTIVATE
  Clicking "Deactivate" prevents the volunteer from signing up for new shifts.
  It does not delete their account or erase their history. Click "Activate" to
  restore their access at any time.

ACTIVITY STATUS
  The volunteer list shows a status badge:
    Active   — logged hours at least the minimum number of times in the
               look-back window (configured in Settings).
    Inactive — account is still on, but they have not logged recently.
    Deactivated — a manager has manually turned off their access.

IMPORTING EXISTING VOLUNTEERS FROM A CSV FILE
  If you are migrating from another system and already have a list of
  volunteer names and emails, you can import them all at once instead of
  entering each person manually.

  1. Open your existing list in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app.
  2. Make sure there are (at minimum) three columns with these exact names in
     the header row:

       first_name    last_name    email

     You may also include:

       phone         notes

     Column names can be upper or lower case. Spaces in column names are fine
     (e.g., "First Name" works the same as "first_name").

  3. Export / Save As CSV (.csv file).
  4. In the portal, click "Import from CSV" just below "Volunteers" in the
     sidebar, then upload your file and click "Upload and Import."
  5. The results page will show:
       - Green: volunteers successfully created.
       - Yellow: rows skipped because that email is already in the system.
       - Red: rows that could not be imported (usually a blank name or email).
  6. Imported volunteers do not have a password yet. Open each person's
     volunteer detail page and use the "Send password-set link" button (or
     ask them to use the Forgot Password flow) to let them set their own
     password.


SHIFTS
------
Shifts are scheduled work sessions that volunteers can sign up for.

CREATING A SHIFT
  1. Click "Shifts" in the sidebar, then "Create New Shift."
  2. Fill in the title, date, start time, end time, activity type, and capacity
     (maximum number of volunteers who can sign up).
  3. Click Save. The shift immediately appears on the volunteer-facing schedule.

MANAGING A SHIFT
  Click any shift title to open its detail page. From there you can:
    - See who has signed up.
    - Edit the shift details.
    - Cancel the shift (removes it from the schedule; existing sign-ups are
      not automatically notified, so send a group email if needed).

Volunteers sign themselves up and can cancel their own sign-ups from their
portal. You do not need to manage sign-ups manually.


HOUR LOGS
---------
Volunteers log their own hours at the kiosk page. Managers can also view,
filter, and delete logs.

VIEWING LOGS
  Click "Hour Logs" in the sidebar. You can filter by volunteer name, date
  range, or activity type. Click "Export CSV" (if available) to download the
  full log list.

DELETING A LOG
  Find the row and click the red Delete button on the right. Deletions are
  permanent.

THE KIOSK PAGE
  The hour-logging kiosk at /portal/log-hours/ does not require a volunteer
  to log in. It is designed to be open in a browser on a shared shelter
  computer. Volunteers walk up, pick their name from the drop-down, fill in
  the form, and click Submit. No passwords involved.

  Bookmark /portal/log-hours/ on the kiosk computer and set it as the
  browser's home page.


SEND GROUP EMAIL
----------------
Use this to send a single message to all, active, or inactive volunteers.
Emails go out from your own Gmail account, so you always control them.

  1. Click "Send Email" in the sidebar.
  2. Choose who to send to from the "Send to" drop-down.
  3. Write a subject and message body, then click "Prepare Email."
  4. The portal shows three copy buttons — BCC addresses, Subject, and Body.
  5. Open Gmail, start a new message, and paste each field:
       - Paste the addresses into the BCC field (not To, so recipients
         don't see each other's email addresses).
       - Paste the subject into the Subject line.
       - Paste the body into the message body.
  6. Review and click Send in Gmail.

The portal keeps a log of every email that was prepared, including the
date, subject, and how many recipients were included.


SETTINGS
--------
Click "Settings" in the sidebar to adjust two things:

INACTIVITY THRESHOLD
  Defines when a volunteer is flagged as "inactive" on the volunteer list.
  The defaults are: fewer than 2 hour logs in the past 90 days.
  Change these numbers and click "Save Settings" to update the rule.

EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS
  Toggle your personal preference for receiving emails when new applications
  arrive. Other managers have their own toggle; changing yours does not affect
  theirs.


TIPS FOR MANAGERS
-----------------
- There is no "are you sure?" prompt for approving applications, so read
  through the submission before clicking Approve or Deny.
- The Notes field on each volunteer detail page is only visible to managers.
  Use it for things like "prefers cat socialization" or "needs a ride."
- If a volunteer loses access to their email and cannot reset their password,
  a Django admin (your IT contact) can log in to the admin panel and set a
  new password manually.
- Shifts, hour logs, and email records are never automatically deleted.
  Everything is kept for your records.


NEED HELP?
----------
This portal was built specifically for Medina County SPCA. For technical
issues — server errors, login problems, email not sending — contact your
web developer.

For questions about the application process or volunteer policies, that
is entirely up to your organization's guidelines. The software just records
what you decide.
