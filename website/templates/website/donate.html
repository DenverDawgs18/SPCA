In-Kind Donations

What we always need

In addition to financial donations, we accept gently used items and new supplies. Drop-offs are accepted 7 days a week, 9 am–4 pm.

  • Dog and cat food (dry and canned)
  • Blankets, towels, and bedding
  • Cleaning supplies (dish soap, bleach, paper towels)
  • Cat litter
  • Toys and enrichment items
  • Leashes, collars, and harnesses
  • Postage stamps and office supplies

Memorial Pavers

Honor a friend, family member, or beloved pet with a personalized engraved brick paver placed permanently on the grounds of our 13-acre property in Seville.

Great for memorials, tributes, or simply showing your love for the animals we care for.

How to order

  1. Download and complete the paver order form (link below).
  2. Mail or email it back to us along with payment.
  3. If you'd like us to notify someone of your order, include their name and contact info.
