In-Kind Donations

What we always need

In addition to financial donations, we accept gently used items and new supplies. Drop-offs are accepted 7 days a week, 9 am–4 pm.

  • Dog and cat food (dry and canned)
  • Blankets, towels, and bedding
  • Cleaning supplies (dish soap, bleach, paper towels)
  • Cat litter
  • Toys and enrichment items
  • Leashes, collars, and harnesses
  • Postage stamps and office supplies
