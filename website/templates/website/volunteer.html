Volunteer With Us

Our volunteers are the heart of the SPCA. With your time, we can do so much more for the animals in our care.

Make a Difference

What volunteers do

From walking dogs to helping at adoption events, there are many ways to give your time, no matter your schedule or what skills you bring.

  • Dog walking and socialization
  • Cat socialization and enrichment
  • Kennel cleaning and maintenance
  • Adoption counseling and meet-and-greets
  • Fundraising and events
  • Administrative support
  • Animal transport
Getting Started

How to become a volunteer

Reach Out

Contact us by phone, email, or the form below to express your interest and tell us a little about yourself.

Approval & Orientation

A volunteer manager will review your inquiry and invite you to our next orientation session.

Schedule Your Shifts

Once approved, coordinate with staff to find shifts that fit your schedule.

Make a Difference

Show up, have fun, and change the lives of the animals who need you most.

Ready to Join?

Get in touch and we'll walk you through the next steps. We'd love to have you.
