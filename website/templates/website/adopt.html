How It Works

The Adoption Process

1

Browse & Choose

Look through our available animals above or come visit us in person. Our staff can help you find the right match for your lifestyle.

2

Submit an Application

Fill out our adoption application online or in person. Applications are reviewed by our team, usually within 1–2 business days.

3

Meet & Greet

Once approved, schedule a time to meet the animal. We encourage the whole family — including resident pets — to come along.

4

Take Them Home

After a successful meet & greet and adoption fee payment, your new family member goes home with you.
