It's Easy

How Adoption Works

Our simple process is designed to help animals and adopters find the right match.

Browse & Visit

Come meet our animals in person during adoption hours (Tue–Sun 12–4 pm) or browse online first.

Complete an Application

Fill out a short adoption application so we can learn a bit about you and your home.

Meet & Greet

Spend time with the animal you're interested in. We encourage family members and current pets to visit too.

Go Home Together

Once approved, pay the adoption fee and take your new family member home — they're ready to go!
