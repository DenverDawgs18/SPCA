Adoption FAQs

Everything you need to know before adopting from Medina County SPCA.

Do I need an appointment to visit the animals?

+
No appointment is needed to come in and meet our animals. Just stop by during adoption hours: Tuesday and Thursday–Sunday, 12–4 pm. We're closed Monday and Wednesday.

What does the adoption fee include?

+
All adoption fees include spay/neuter surgery, age-appropriate vaccinations, and microchipping. Dogs are also heartworm tested. These services together typically cost significantly more than our adoption fee.

Can I bring my current pets to meet a potential new animal?

+
Absolutely — we encourage it! We have a safe, neutral area where you can introduce your current dog to a potential adoptee. Please call ahead so we can prepare. Cats are introduced more carefully on a case-by-case basis.

Do you adopt to people outside of Medina County?

+
Yes! We adopt to residents of any Ohio county and evaluate out-of-state applications on a case-by-case basis. We just ask that you visit in person before finalizing an adoption.

How long does the adoption process take?

+
Many adoptions are completed same-day. After you fill out the application and we've done a brief review, you can usually take your new pet home the same day — as long as the animal is already spayed/neutered. Some younger animals may need to stay with us a bit longer before going home.

Is there a return policy?

+
Yes. If the adoption isn't working out for any reason, please contact us — we never want an animal to end up in an uncertain situation. We'd rather have them returned to us than rehomed privately without our knowledge.

Can I adopt as a senior citizen?

+
Of course! We welcome senior adopters and may offer discounted fees for adult animals. Please ask a staff member when you visit.

Do you have animals other than dogs and cats?

+
Occasionally we have rabbits, guinea pigs, birds, or other small animals available. Check our available animals page or call us to ask about current inventory.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help. Reach out by phone or message.
