About Medina County SPCA

Over 110 years of humane care, advocacy, and community service in Medina County, Ohio.

Our Mission

Committed to the humane care of animals

The Medina County SPCA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to the humane treatment, care, and adoption of companion animals in Medina County, Ohio. We believe every animal deserves a safe, loving home.

We operate a 13-acre facility in Seville, Ohio, and serve thousands of animals each year through our adoption, foster, and surrender programs. We are a fully independent, locally operated organization with no affiliation to the national ASPCA or HSUS.
