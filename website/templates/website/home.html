Our Story

A community shelter for over 110 years

Since 1913, the Medina County SPCA has been the backbone of humane animal care in our region. Located on 13 acres in Seville, Ohio, our facility provides a safe haven for dogs, cats, and small animals in need.

We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that relies entirely on donations, adoptions, and the generous time of our volunteers. Every animal who comes through our doors receives proper veterinary care, socialization, and love.
