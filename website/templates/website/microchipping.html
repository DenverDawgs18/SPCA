Microchipping Services

One of the simplest things you can do to protect your pet. A microchip gives lost animals a way home.

Protect Your Pet

Fast, safe, and affordable

A microchip is a permanent form of identification about the size of a grain of rice, implanted just beneath the skin between your pet's shoulder blades. When a lost pet is scanned at a shelter or vet clinic, the chip reveals your contact information.

Collars and tags can fall off. A microchip lasts a lifetime.

$25 Per pet, includes chip + registration
By Appointment Monday through Friday

What's included

  • ISO-compatible 15-digit microchip implantation
  • Registration in a national pet recovery database
  • Documentation for your records

Already Chipped?

Keep your registration current

A microchip only works if your contact information is up to date in the registry. If you've moved or changed your phone number, log in to your pet's registry account and update it right away.

Not sure which registry your pet is in? Use the AAHA Universal Pet Microchip Lookup to search by chip number.
