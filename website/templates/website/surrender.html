Pet Surrender Services

We understand that life circumstances change. We're here to help, with no judgment.

Please contact us before coming in.

Surrenders are by appointment only so we can properly prepare for your animal's arrival. Call us at (330) 723-7722 or use the contact form below.

We accept dogs, cats, and small animals

Our intake process is designed to be as stress-free as possible for you and your pet. A surrender fee may apply to help offset the cost of care. No animal is turned away due to inability to pay. Please reach out and we will work something out.

Before surrendering, we encourage you to consider:

  • Reaching out to friends, family, or breed-specific rescue groups
  • Posting on local community boards or social media
  • Our foster network (sometimes a temporary break is all a family needs)

What to bring

  • Any vet records you have
  • A favorite toy or blanket (helps with the transition)
  • Information about the animal's behavior, likes, dislikes, and history
