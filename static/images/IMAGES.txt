MEDINA COUNTY SPCA — IMAGES NEEDED
=====================================
Drop files into their listed paths. The site falls back to a colored
placeholder automatically if a file is missing — no code changes needed.

HOW THE LOGO WORKS
------------------
  static/images/logo.svg          <- drag your logo here, done.
  static/images/logo.png          <- PNG also works (update src in base.html if using .png)

  The site shows a navy "M" circle until a logo file is present.


SITE-WIDE IMAGES
-----------------
  static/images/hero-home.jpg
    Homepage hero background. Wide landscape shot of animals or the facility.
    Ideal dimensions: 1920 x 640 px. Shot will be darkened by overlay, so
    vibrant colors show through nicely (a dog or cat looking at camera works great).

  static/images/about-mission.jpg
    Shown on the homepage "About" split section and the About page.
    A warm photo of staff or volunteers with animals. 680 x 400 px.

  static/images/about-facility.jpg
    Exterior or aerial shot of the 13-acre Seville property. 640 x 400 px.

  static/images/facility-inside.jpg
    Interior kennel or cat room photo for the "Visit Our Facility" section. 640 x 420 px.

  static/images/volunteer-action.jpg
    A volunteer walking a dog or socializing with cats at the shelter. 640 x 420 px.

  static/images/foster-home.jpg
    A foster family at home with a foster pet (couch, living room setting). 640 x 420 px.

  static/images/donations.jpg
    Bags of food or cleaning supplies being dropped off at the shelter. 640 x 420 px.

  static/images/pavers.jpg
    The memorial paver walkway on the shelter grounds. 640 x 420 px.

  static/images/microchipping.jpg
    A staff member or vet microchipping a calm dog or cat. 640 x 420 px.

  static/images/cruelty-response.jpg
    A tasteful rescue or care image (animal being gently held or examined). 640 x 420 px.

TEAM / STAFF PHOTOS
--------------------
  static/images/team/
    Name files after each person: firstname-lastname.jpg  (e.g. jane-smith.jpg)
    These are referenced in about.html once real staff info is filled in.
    Format: square, 200 x 200 px minimum, friendly headshot.


NOTES
------
- All formats accepted: .jpg, .png, .svg, .webp
- Max recommended file size: 500 KB for page images, 100 KB for team photos
- The logo (logo.svg) is the only file that causes an instant visible change
  on every page — prioritize getting that one in first.
- Run "python manage.py collectstatic" after adding images if deploying to Fly.io.
